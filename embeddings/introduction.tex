\chapter{\texorpdfstring{Embeddings in \boldmath${\la_b}$}{Embeddings in the boxed lambda calculus}}
\label{chap:embeddings}

This chapter discusses the modal embeddings of the call-by-name \lc (\lan), and the call-by-value \lc (\lav) 
into the shared target, call-by-box \lc (\lab).
We also give propositions about the embeddings, which will be proven in Agda later.
These propositions state that reduction and evaluation are preserved by the embeddings, 
and have been proven several times in different contexts, for example, using linear logic or thunks \cite{EhrhardGuerrieri2016, MARAIST1999175, HATCLIFF_DANVY_1997}.
However, \lab is a refined version of \labox \cite{espritosanto_et_al:LIPIcs:2019:10525}.
We formalise these embeddings and prove their propositions in Agda.
