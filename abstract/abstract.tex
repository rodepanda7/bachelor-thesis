\begin{abstract}
% Brief outline of research questions, results.
% (The preferred size of an abstract is one paragraph or one page of text.)
This thesis presents a formalisation of the boxed \lc (\lab) in the formal proof assistant Agda.
This calculus is a refinement of \labox, a modal \lc that is a fragment of \textsf{IS4} \cite{espritosanto_et_al:LIPIcs:2019:10525}.
Girard's and Gödel's embeddings are translations from the call-by-name \lc (\lan) and the call-by-value \lc (\lav) into the shared target, call-by-box \lc (\lab).
We implement these embedding in Agda.
We then proof that Girard's embedding preserves reduction and evaluation, and that Gödel's embedding preserves reduction.
Preservation of Gödel's embedding is not proven in Agda, due to time limitations, but it is shown in \cite{espritosanto_et_al:LIPIcs:2019:10525}.
\end{abstract}
